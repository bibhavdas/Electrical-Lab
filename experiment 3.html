Study of R-L-C series circuit

Objective: To measure the voltage drop of the individual components, total circuit current and power factor of R L C series circuit.

Theory: The three are shown in figure joined in series across an A.C. supply of rms voltage V.

Let
VR = I * R = voltage drop across R.
VL = I * X_L = Voltage drop across L, X_L = 2πfL.
VC = I * X_C = Voltage drop across C, X_C = 1 / 2πfC.
Its phase angle is ø = tan -1[(X_L - X_C)/R].

Procedure:
  1. The circuit is made in the circuit diagram.
  2. The variac should be kept in zero voltage position, the AC supply is switched ON.
  3. The applied voltage is varied using the variac & corresponding value of current and voltage are noted.
  4. The readings of various voltages are tabulated till rated voltage (210V) of components is reached.
Circuit Diagram:
Precaution:
  1. 1) All the meters should be taken in proper range.
  2. 2) Before connecting the meter in the circuits check zero settings.
  3. 3) The current, voltage are noted when values settle to a steady value.
  4. 4) All joints should be tightly connected.
  5. 5) Voltage should not exceed 210 V.
  6. 6) The supply is switched off after experiment is over.
