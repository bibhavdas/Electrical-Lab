Title: Tungsten & Carbon Filament Lamp

Objective:

To study the characteristic voltage-resistance-current, resistance-voltage & voltage-power of tungsten & carbon filament lamps.

Theory:

Resistance of any material varies with temperature. The resistance of conductor is dependent on collision processes within the wire. The resistance is expected to increase with temperature since there will be more collisions.

For the temperature range that is not too great, this resistance variation can be represented approximately as a linear function:

$$ R_T = R_0 [1 + \alpha(T - T_a)] $$

The temperature co-efficient of Tungsten and Carbon are \( +0.0045 \) & \( -0.0005 \) respectively.

Precaution:

1. All the meters should be taken in proper range.

2. Before connecting the meter in the circuits check zero settings.

3. The current, voltage are noted when values settle to a steady value.

4. All joints should be tightly connected.

5. Supply is switched off after the experiment is over.

Procedure:

1. The circuit is made as shown in the circuit diagram.

2. With the Tungsten lamp / Carbon lamp in the circuit and the variac in zero voltage position, the supply is switched on.

3. The applied voltage is varied using the variac and corresponding value of voltage, current and power are noted.

4. The change of the brightness of the lamp with variation of voltage is observed.

5. The reading of various voltages are tabulated till the rated voltage (210V) of the lamp is reached.

6. The steps above (2-5) are repeated replacing tungsten lamp by the Carbon lamp.

Conclusion:

From the graphs and observation table, we conclude that:

1. For the Tungsten Lamp (Metal), the resistance increases as the voltage (and temperature) increases. This indicates a Positive Temperature Coefficient (PTC).

2. For the Carbon Lamp (Non-metal/Semiconductor), the resistance decreases as the voltage increases. This indicates a Negative Temperature Coefficient (NTC).
